About the data

Beatmapsets are counted by unique ID. Favourites are counted once across modes. Playcount and favourites are cumulative at collection time, not additions within 2026. Genre and language count beatmapsets; songs are grouped by title and artist.

A new mapper first hosted a Ranked/Approved beatmapset in 2026, across all modes. Loved maps and guest difficulties alone do not qualify. Country, newcomer and search filters apply to beatmapset hosts, including the scope of the GDer table. Missing profile data remains unknown.
